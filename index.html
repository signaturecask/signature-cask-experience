Our Story

Born beneath the coastal winds of St Andrews, Scotland, Signature Cask bridges pure liquid history with sensory theatre.

Our flagship release breathes new life into a unique 16-Year-Old single cask Inchgower profile. We harvested the exact timber from Cask #812264B to engineer our bespoke smoking staves.

When ignited tableside, the physical wood structure uncoils vanilla, sea spray, and deep oak tannins back directly into the dram—closing an elemental circle broken nearly two decades ago.

The Smoking Ritual

Follow these precision steps to correctly awaken the original 2009 cask DNA:

Step I

Prepare the Dram

Pour exactly 50ml of your Inchgower 16-Year-Old Legacy into a premium luxury tumbler or rocks glass.

Step II

Load the Cask Chips

Place your wooden top-glass smoker directly onto the rim of the tumbler. Add a small pinch of the authentic Cask #812264B wood chips into the central metal mesh chimney basket.

Step III

Ignite with Precision

Hold your professional butane torch lighter 2 inches away. Direct the flame straight down onto the wood chips for 3 to 5 seconds until they ember and thick smoke cascades down into the glass.

Step IV

Infuse and Serve

Leave the smoker lid seated on top of the glass for 15-20 seconds. This traps the heavy oak tannins and vanilla oils, infusing the whisky before presentation.

Bespoke Smoked Cocktails

Utilizing original oak smoking chips sourced directly from Cask #812264B:

The 2009 Cask Old Fashioned
50ml Inchgower 16 Legacy, 1 Bar Spoon Demerara Sugar, 2 Dashes Angostura Bitters. Infuse glass using your cask smoking chips for 20 seconds before expressing the citrus peel.

The St Andrews Coastal Sour
