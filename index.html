Reclaiming Cask DNA

Born beneath the coastal winds of St Andrews, Scotland, Signature Cask bridges pure liquid history with sensory theatre.

Our flagship release breathes new life into a unique 16-Year-Old single cask Inchgower profile. Rather than letting the historic wood sleep forever, we systematically harvested the exact timber from Cask #812264B to engineer our bespoke smoking staves.

When ignited tableside, the physical wood structure uncoils vanilla, sea spray, and deep oak tannins back directly into the dram—closing an elemental circle broken nearly two decades ago.

The 2009 Cask Old Fashioned

50ml Inchgower 16 Legacy, 1 Bar Spoon Demerara, 2 Dashes Angostura.

🔥 Smoke Activation: Trap embers from your Cask #812264B oak chips inside the glass for 20 seconds prior to expressing the orange twist.

The St Andrews Coastal Sour

50ml Inchgower Whisky, 25ml Fresh Lemon, 15ml Honey Syrup, Egg White.

🔥 Smoke Activation: Dry shake, strain over clean ice cube, flash-smoke with the hand smoker directly over the foam top before presentation.
