Our Story

Born beneath the coastal winds of St Andrews, Scotland, Signature Cask bridges pure liquid history with sensory theatre.

Our flagship release breathes new life into a unique 16-Year-Old single cask Inchgower profile. We harvested the exact timber from Cask #812264B to engineer our bespoke smoking staves.

When ignited tableside, the wood uncoils vanilla, sea spray, and deep oak tannins back directly into the dram—closing an elemental circle broken nearly two decades ago.

Smoked Cocktail Recipes

The 2009 Cask Old Fashioned
50ml Inchgower 16 Legacy, 1 Bar Spoon Demerara, 2 Dashes Angostura. Smoke with Cask #812264B oak chips for 20 seconds before serving.

The St Andrews Coastal Sour
50ml Inchgower Whisky, 25ml Fresh Lemon, 15ml Honey Syrup, Egg White. Flash-smoke over the foam top before presentation.
